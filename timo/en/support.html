Common questions

Can I use Timo without signing in?

Yes. Plans, focus, memos, countdowns, and file backups work locally. Google / Apple sign-in provides access to optional cloud backups.

Do cloud backups sync automatically?

No. Upload manually under Settings → Backup & data → Cloud backup. On another device, sign into the same account, review the backup, and confirm restoration. Restoration replaces local content; export a file backup first. Cloud features need a network connection and available services.

How do I export or erase local data?

Open Settings → Backup & data to export, restore from a file, or clear local data. Exported files may include private content and photos; keep them secure. Clearing local content does not delete cloud backups or exported files.

How do I delete my account and cloud backups?

Open Settings → Sign-in & account, choose Delete Timo account, and verify your identity. Successful deletion removes the account and all cloud backups. Local content and exported files remain. Retry or contact support if deletion fails. Signing out does not delete an account.

Why did a reminder not appear?

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Timo, the in-app reminder settings, and the plan time. Focus modes, scheduled summaries, and system delivery limits may affect reminders. Local notifications cannot guarantee delivery in every system state.

How do I add home-screen widgets?

On iOS 17 and later, touch and hold an empty area of your home screen and add Timo widgets. Settings → Home-screen widgets includes previews and instructions. iOS controls refresh timing. Plans, memo excerpts, and photos may be visible to people who can see your screen.

Does the three-day trial charge automatically?

No. First use starts 72 continuous hours of access without a purchase. Basic features remain free afterwards. If you actively choose a monthly or yearly subscription, Apple charges immediately and renews it automatically; the system confirmation shows the local price.

How do I restore Plus or cancel renewal?

Choose Restore purchases in Settings → Timo Plus with the Apple account used for your purchase. Cancel renewal in system subscription settings; paid access lasts until the period ends. Deleting your Timo account or uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Does the memo lock encrypt my backups?

No. The lock protects access inside the app; it does not encrypt backups. The numeric PIN remains free, with gesture and biometric options during Plus or the app trial. Keep your PIN and exported files safe.

How do I manage analytics and ad privacy?

Turn off Help improve Timo under Settings → Data & privacy. Analytics is off by default. When ads are enabled and regional rules require it, Ad privacy options appears on that page. Analytics consent and advertising choices are managed separately.
